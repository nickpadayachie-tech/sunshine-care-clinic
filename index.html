About Sunshine Health Care

Your neighbourhood clinic under one friendly roof

Sunshine Health Care is a Clinic offering warm, professional and affordable healthcare for every stage of life, from little ones to grandparents. Our space at Gateway Mall in Carletonville is run by SR Sylvia P Mngadi, an experienced Private Nurse Practitioner.
