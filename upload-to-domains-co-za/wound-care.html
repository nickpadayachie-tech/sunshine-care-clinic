Wound Clinic

Focused wound care with consistent follow up

We care for a range of ulcers and pressure injuries with skill and attention, so your wound gets the consistent follow up it needs.

Conditions we care for

  • Pressure sores
  • Pressure ulcers
  • Diabetic ulcers
  • Venous ulcers
  • Arterial ulcers
  • Diabetic wounds
  • Negative pressure application

Delivered with care

Wound care is provided by a qualified Nurse Practitioner with consistent follow up at each visit. Your practitioner will advise on ongoing management and on when referral to another healthcare provider is appropriate.
