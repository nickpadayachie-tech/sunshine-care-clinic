What is IV drip therapy?

IV drip therapy delivers fluids and vitamins directly into the bloodstream through a small intravenous drip. At Sunshine Health Care we offer a menu of vitamin enriched drips designed for different everyday needs.

  • Hydration support
  • Energy and fatigue support
  • Immune booster blend
  • Skin glow and wellness blends

Drips are administered in our clinic space at Gateway Mall, inside Phelang Pharmacy, Carletonville by a qualified professional.

Which drip is right for you?

Whether you need simple hydration, a boost after a busy stretch, or extra care before a big day, there is a Vitalize drip in the range for you.

  • Hydrating Drip — R350
  • Hangover Drip — R600
  • Burnout & Fatigue Drip — R700
  • Immune Booster Drip — R800
  • Skin Glow Drip — R900
