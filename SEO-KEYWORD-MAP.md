## Local SEO signals used

- Consistent NAP (name, address, phone) in footer, top bar, contact page and JSON-LD
  on every page.
- Phone links (`tel:+27812481247`) and WhatsApp links (`wa.me/27792131692`) throughout.
- Google Maps embed (lazy-loaded with a descriptive `title`) and a "directions" link on
  the Contact page.
- Google review link surfaced on the Contact page.

## Usage notes

- Write naturally; never keyword-stuff. The keyword should appear in the title, the H1,
  and at least once in the first paragraph.
- Update this map whenever a page is re-titled or a new service is added.
