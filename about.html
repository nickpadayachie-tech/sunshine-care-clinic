Clinic overview

Sunshine Health Care is a clinic at Gateway Mall, inside Phelang Pharmacy, 2 Osmium Street, Carletonville, Gauteng. We offer warm, professional and affordable healthcare for every stage of life — from little ones to grandparents.

Every visit starts with listening. We take the time to understand your needs, answer your questions, and guide you on the next best step for your health.

Our services include IV drip therapy and vitamin drips, baby wellness and immunisation, basic assessments, family clinic care and wound care.
